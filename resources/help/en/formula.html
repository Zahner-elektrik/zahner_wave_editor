Formula segments

A formula returns the segment value at time t. By default, t starts at zero for each segment repetition.

Enable Global time to use time from the beginning of the document.

Constants and operators

Constants: pi, e. Operators include + - * / ^, comparisons, and parentheses.

Functions

sin cos tan asin acos atan sinh cosh tanh sqrt exp ln log log2 log10 abs sign min max rint

Examples

  • sin(2*pi*10*t) - 10-cycle-per-time-unit sine
  • 0.5 + 2*exp(-t) - exponential decay with offset
  • max(-1, min(1, 4*t-1)) - limited ramp

Functions use radians. Invalid expressions are marked in the editor and are not committed.

Powers: Write t^2, for example. The editor preserves ^ as the formula operator.
